WIKIMEDIA POLICY SEMANTIC-REVIEW REVIEWER EXECUTION SPECIFICATION
=================================================================

Authority: Decision 147 fresh M45-07 execution path, superseding Decision 103's
reviewer-launch and mechanical-isolation provisions. This document is bound
into the wikimedia-policy-reviewer-contract.v1 commitment (executionSpecSha256)
and into the role-input manifest of every prepare-review invocation.

1. Reviewer scope

The reviewer receives exactly two inputs: the fixed reviewer prompt
(scripts/policy-baseline-review/reviewer-prompt.txt) and the five complete
decoded public policy bodies from the role-input bundle
(.local/m45/policy-baseline-review/role-input/body-01.bin through body-05.bin)
with their manifest commitments. No candidate, predecessor, identity, user,
environment, capture, or other data is provided.

The reviewer performs NO retrieval of its own. All policy retrieval is
performed by the prepare-review command, whose matching retrieval must equal
the bootstrap capture exactly (ordered hashes, byte counts, total, and the
ordered URL-sequence commitment) before the role input is promoted. The
reviewer never fetches, never opens any URL, and never inspects any file other
than the role input it is assigned.

2. Role output

The reviewer returns exactly one closed role-output object matching
scripts/policy-baseline-review/role-output.schema.json: schema, version,
captureSha256, semanticReviewRetrievalSha256, reviewerContractSha256, and one
closed outcome (no-material-change or exactly one of the seven material-change
categories). No prose, no body content, no extra fields, no justification.
The reviewer never computes or claims a SHA-256; the commitments are provided
in the framing and are copied unchanged into the role output.

3. Delivery

The reviewer does not write any file. The orchestrator writes the returned
output to the preassigned vacant path
.local/m45/policy-baseline-review/role-result/role-output.json with create-new
semantics, mode 0600, at most 4 KiB, and only canonical schema-valid JSON is
accepted.

4. Finalization

The trusted finalize command re-reads the capture and role-input bundles,
requires the preassigned role-output.json to exist, revalidates every capture,
retrieval, role-input, and role-output commitment, constructs the
wikimedia-policy-semantic-review-role-result.v1 authority, removes the entire
temporary review root whole-file, proves the root and every staging sibling
absent, and only then, provided the closed outcome is exactly
no-material-change, emits the wikimedia-policy-semantic-review.v1 and the
tracked wikimedia-policy-baseline.v1 authorities. Authority emission always
follows body removal and absence proof. Any invalid, raced, or unexpected
state stops without deletion.

5. Isolation

The reviewer assignment forbids reading .local/ custody, other repository
private state, database configuration, or environment values. The reviewer
receives only the public policy bodies and the fixed contract, emits only the
closed role output, and has no filesystem or retrieval capability over the
custody path. The finalizer revalidates all commitments before any authority
is emitted.
